ABOUT THE PROJECT

A complete digital restaurant workflow

Scan2Serve replaces traditional manual ordering with a connected digital workflow from table scanning to kitchen preparation and final billing.

QR Table Ordering

Customers scan the QR code assigned to their table and directly access the digital menu.

Digital Cart

Customers can browse menu items, manage quantities and place orders directly from their phones.

Kitchen Management

Kitchen staff can view incoming orders and manage their preparation status.

Digital Billing

Billing staff can manage bills, payments and completed restaurant orders.
